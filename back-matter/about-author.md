# About the Author

John F. Gnazzo is a software developer, author, songwriter and podcaster based in Eden Prairie, Minnesota. His writing brings practical software engineering together with an interest in explaining difficult ideas through concrete examples.

Building Mobile Apps with C# 15 connects those interests through Gnazzo's World, a teaching application built around books, music, podcasts and custom-song requests. The project provides a setting for the questions that make an application dependable: how it preserves work, handles uncertainty, protects ownership and recovers after change.

John's creative work appears under the DGreatGnazzini imprints. He lives in Eden Prairie with his wife, Shelly. More about his books, music and podcasts is available at https://johngnazzo.com.
